Refund Policy

We want you to be confident in your subscription. This Policy explains when refunds are available, how to request one, and what happens after cancellation.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you’re new to Vibe Travel Stack, we offer a full refund within 14 days of your first subscription charge, no questions asked. This applies to your first subscription period only.

To request a refund within this window, email support@vibetravelstack.com from the email address associated with your account. Include your subscription start date. We process refunds within 5 business days; the credit may take 5–10 additional business days to appear depending on your card issuer or bank.

2. After the 14-Day Window

  • No prorated refunds. After the initial 14 days, monthly subscription payments are non-refundable.
  • Cancel anytime. You can cancel from your account dashboard or by emailing support. Cancellation stops future charges; you retain access until the end of the current billing period.
  • Reactivation. You can resubscribe at any time. The 14-day money-back guarantee applies only to your first subscription, not subsequent reactivations.

3. Exceptions and Disputes

We may, in our discretion, grant refunds outside the 14-day window in cases of:

  • Service outages. Extended downtime that prevents you from using the Service for a substantial portion of your billing period.
  • Billing errors. Duplicate charges, unauthorized charges, or incorrect billing amounts.
  • Account compromise. Charges made by an unauthorized party using your account, where you reported the issue promptly.

Email support@vibetravelstack.com with details. We respond within 3 business days.

4. Chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please contact us first — we resolve nearly every issue directly. Initiating a chargeback (disputing a charge with your card issuer) before contacting us may result in account suspension while the dispute is reviewed. We retain the right to dispute fraudulent chargebacks.

5. Promotional Pricing & Coupons

Subscriptions purchased with promotional pricing (discount codes, partner offers, free trials) are subject to the same 14-day guarantee on the first paid period. Free trial periods are not refundable since no charge has been made. After a free trial converts to a paid subscription, the 14-day window begins from the first paid charge.

6. Third-Party Services

eSIM data plans, VPN sessions, and translation usage consumed through Vibe Travel Stack are powered by third-party partners and are typically not refundable once provisioned or used. The 14-day money-back guarantee covers the subscription cost itself; it does not entitle you to a refund of partner data charges or usage already consumed.

7. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email support@vibetravelstack.com from your account email.
  2. Subject: “Refund request — [your account email]”
  3. Include your subscription start date and (optional) reason.
  4. We confirm receipt within 1 business day and process eligible refunds within 5 business days.

8. Contact

Questions about this Policy: support@vibetravelstack.com.

Important disclosure. Vibe Travel Stack is a software service. eSIM data plans, VPN tunneling, and translation services are powered by third-party partners. Travel-related information is for general convenience and is not financial, legal, medical, or immigration advice.
