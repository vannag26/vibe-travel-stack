DMCA Notice

Vibe Technologies LLC respects the intellectual property rights of others and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (“DMCA”). This page explains how to report alleged copyright infringement on vibetravelstack.com or services we operate, and how to submit a counter-notice if your content was removed.

1. Filing a Notice of Claimed Infringement

If you believe content available through our Service infringes your copyright, please send a written notice to our designated DMCA agent that includes all of the following (per 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)):

  1. A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or, if multiple works, a representative list).
  3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, including the URL or other location with sufficient detail for us to find it.
  4. Your contact information: name, postal address, telephone number, and email.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

3. What Happens After We Receive a Valid Notice

We will:

  • Review the notice for completeness;
  • If valid, remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material;
  • Notify the user who posted the material;
  • Document the action for our records.

4. Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was removed in error or as a result of misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice to our designated agent containing:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material that has been removed and the location at which it appeared before removal.
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, postal address, telephone number, and a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or, if outside the US, any judicial district in which we may be found), and that you will accept service from the original notifier or their agent.

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we may restore the removed material in 10–14 business days unless the original notifier files a court action seeking a restraining order.

5. Repeat Infringers

We have a policy of terminating accounts of users we determine to be repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances.

6. Misrepresentations

Knowingly making a material misrepresentation in a DMCA notice or counter-notice may subject you to liability for damages, including costs and attorneys’ fees, under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f). Please consult an attorney if you are unsure whether material is infringing.
