Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Vibe Technologies LLC uses cookies and similar technologies on vibetravelstack.com and within the Vibe Travel Stack application. Read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let the site remember information about your visit — like your login state, language preferences, or items in a cart — and help us understand how the Service is used. Similar technologies (local storage, pixel tags, web beacons) work in comparable ways and are covered by this Policy.

3. How to Control Cookies

You can manage cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner. When you first visit, you’ll see a banner letting you accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • Account settings. Logged-in users can adjust analytics opt-in from the Privacy section of their dashboard.
  • Browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will break login, billing, and core functionality.
  • Do Not Track. We respect Do Not Track signals where technically feasible by suppressing analytics cookies.
