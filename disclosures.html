Disclosures & Disclaimers

This page consolidates the disclosures that govern your use of Vibe Travel Stack. Read alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Nature of the Service

Vibe Travel Stack is a software service operated by Vibe Technologies LLC. We provide a unified interface to four travel utilities: real-time currency exchange, eSIM activation, VPN protection, and translation. We are not a bank, money services business, telecommunications carrier, internet service provider, or licensed advisor of any kind.

2. Third-Party Service Dependencies

The functional components of Vibe Travel Stack are powered by third-party partners and APIs. Service availability, performance, pricing, and policies of these partners are outside our direct control.
  • eSIM data plans are provisioned by partners such as Airalo (or successors). Coverage, speeds, device compatibility (including IMEI restrictions), and plan availability are determined by these partners and the underlying mobile network operators.
  • VPN tunneling is provided by partners such as Mullvad / Proton (or successors). Server availability, connection speed, and jurisdictional reach depend on the partner’s infrastructure and policies. We do not log your VPN browsing activity, but partners’ own logging policies apply.
  • Translation is powered by AI models (Workers AI Llama or other partners). Translation accuracy varies by language pair and context. Do not rely on translations for legal, medical, or critical communications without independent verification.
  • Currency exchange rates are provided via the RateWire API operated by Vibe Technologies LLC. Rates are aggregated from market data providers and may differ from rates available at banks, money services, or other providers. Rates are for informational purposes; we are not a money transmitter or currency exchange business.

3. Not Financial Advice

Information presented in the FX widget, including exchange rates, conversion calculations, and rate alerts, is for general informational purposes only. It is not investment advice, currency trading advice, or any other form of financial advice. 4. Not Legal, Tax, Immigration, or Medical Advice

Regulations change frequently and vary by jurisdiction and individual circumstances. Always verify current requirements with:

  • Healthcare providers for vaccination and medical travel requirements
